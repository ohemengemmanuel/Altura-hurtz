The studio

A room built around your voice

You record in an isolated vocal booth while the engineer runs the session from the control room on the other side of the glass. Every take is captured through a Universal Audio Apollo interface, checked on KRK monitors, then edited, tuned and balanced until the record is ready for streaming platforms.
